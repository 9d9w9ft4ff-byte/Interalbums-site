The Booth

Every listener gets a Booth — not a profile. A collection of things they actually lived through. Ticket stubs. Vinyl sleeves. Journal pages. Cassette connections. Conduits get a radio booth. Artists get a studio. Each one earned, each one different.

Fan: ticket stubs, vinyl, cassettes, Genuine Articles Artist: studio texture, notebook pages, microphone Conduit: mixing board, frequency card, broadcast archive Fan progression: Explorer → Dweller → Steward → Conduit

W-LPX & Concasts

Earned radio. Conduits — fans who've demonstrated taste and contribution — host Concasts on W-LPX. Recurring Frequencies, Deep Cuts, Discovery Nights. Listeners earn Concast Stubs. Conduits earn ad revenue. Every broadcast builds a scene.

5 Concast types: Frequency, Deep Cut, Discovery Night, Anniversary, Collab Cast Recurring schedules auto-generate weekly Concast Stubs issued to every listener Conduits earn 50% of ad revenue per read

The Echo System

After every Reflection Circle, fans submit Echoes — ideas, requests, interpretations the music inspired. Echoes incubate. They gather support. They gain momentum. Artists respond. Conduits receive their own Echoes. Participation becomes consequential.

Echo types: Remix, Collab, Vinyl, Event, Expansion Time-decayed momentum ranking in The Nexus Artist + Conduit response threads Echo Observatory in The Signal
