The Community

People Orbit
Moments.
Not Each Other.

Every other platform built a social graph. We built a social topography. You don't collect connections here. You accumulate shared history — through the Occasions you attend, the Echoes you leave, the Campfires you gather around after the music ends.

Familiar Faces — recognized only through shared moments

Campfires — ephemeral rooms that form after events, then dissolve

Echo Threads — closed rooms of witnesses building meaning together

Serendipity — one reveal at a time. Never a directory.

No follower counts. No vanity metrics. No social graph.

How It Works

You show up to moments.

Attend an Occasion. Listen to a Concast. Enter an Interalbum. The platform tracks your presence — not for algorithms, but for history.

Familiar Faces emerge.

When your path crosses someone else's enough times, they become a Familiar Face. Not a friend. Not a follower. Someone you recognize in the room.

Serendipity opens a door.

The system offers one introduction at a time — someone who experienced the same moment similarly to you. You reveal. They reveal. A conversation opens. If you pass, it's gone.

The Conduit Path

The Most
Dedicated
Fans Earn
The Right
To Broadcast.

Conduits are not influencers. They're cultural operators — a mix of DJ, journalist, curator, marketer, and facilitator. The path is earned through 90 days of demonstrated participation. It cannot be purchased.

1

Explorer

First contact. Free. Begin attending Occasions and exploring the Albrary.

2

Dweller

Returns intentionally. Submits Echoes. Builds a presence in the platform.

3

Steward

Active contributor. Supports others' Echoes. Attends Concasts. Builds genuine history.

→

Conduit

90 days as Steward + 7 activity thresholds. The system detects it. You earn it.
